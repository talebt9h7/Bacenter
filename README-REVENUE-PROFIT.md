# Revenue + Profit v33

Added dedicated admin pages:
- `/admin/revenue`
- `/admin/profit`

Both use the Finance permission section.

Revenue:
- Product revenue = subtotal - discount
- Delivery collections are shown separately
- Cancelled/returned orders excluded
- Revenue by order source

Profit:
- Product revenue
- Historical purchase-cost COGS from sold-item snapshots
- Gross profit and margin
- Operating expenses
- Net profit and margin
- Unknown historical costs are not invented

Navigation now includes Revenue, Profit, and Finance & Expenses.
